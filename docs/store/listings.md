# Google Play listings

## en-US
**Title:** SMS Forwarder

**Short description:** Forward incoming SMS to your phone, email, Telegram or webhook.

**Full description:**
Forward incoming text messages to destinations you choose. Send a copy to another phone, your Telegram chat, an HTTPS webhook or an email address through your SMTP account.

Choose light or dark mode, pause forwarding when needed, and send a test message to check your setup. Pro adds multiple destinations, filters, notification forwarding, routing rules, quiet hours, delivery reports and an ad-free experience.

SMS forwarding requires SMS permissions. Notification forwarding requires optional notification access. Carrier charges may apply. Messages go to your configured destinations, not a developer-operated server.
